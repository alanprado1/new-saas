    Why this stops looking generated

    The palette was never the problem. These habits were, and every option here drops them.

    • No glow, haze or gradient fills. The purple radial wash, coloured box-shadows and gradient progress bars go. Depth comes from three graphite steps and one soft shadow.
    • Accent does jobs only. Primary buttons, selection, the active tab, furigana and the playback line. Never borders on every control.
    • Your scene art carries the colour. Cards show the artwork undimmed; the frame around it stays quiet.
    • Meaning colours are fixed. Green correct, red wrong, amber checkpoint on every screen, so flashcard ratings and lesson feedback agree.
    • Real type for Japanese. BIZ UDPGothic (a Morisawa universal-design face) for interface Japanese, and Klee One, a textbook-style face, for words you study, so kana and kanji show their handwritten forms.
    • One signature: the spoken line. A thin accent underline tracks audio in the scene player and flashcards. That's the only ornament.

    When you pick one

    1. Tokens in globals.css; the five accents rewritten in lib/themes.ts, switcher kept.
    2. Course: remap --course-* in the two Busuu CSS modules to the dark tokens. Layout untouched.
    3. Shell: dock (or sidebar) navigation and a Home route; inline rgba(255,255,255,…) styles in Library, Study and the Scene player become tokens.
    4. Fonts self-hosted with next/font; tsc, tests, and screenshots of changed screens on :3100.
