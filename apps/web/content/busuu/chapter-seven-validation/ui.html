Creation/readiness teaching

Compare three things that can newly come into existence.

新しい店ができました。友達ができました。畑でトマトができました。

A new shop has been built. I made a friend. Tomatoes have grown in the field.

できる can mean that something new comes into existence: a shop is built, a friendship forms or produce grows. With a language or an activity, it can express ability. Context identifies the meaning; the thing that comes into existence is marked with が.

流

Static shape study · animation replacement

Recognize the water radical 氵 on the left of 流. Static shape aid replacing animation; it makes no historical derivation or stroke-order claim.

Meaning: flow; let flow

  • ながれる — Kun reading with れる: intransitive flow.
  • ながす — Kun reading with す: transitive let flow/flush.
  • りゅう — On reading in 流行（りゅうこう）.
