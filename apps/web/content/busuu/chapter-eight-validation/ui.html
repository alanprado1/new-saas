Reason-based expectations

Read the connections before はず.

彼は来るはずです。この店は静かなはずです。彼女は先生のはずです。

He should come. This shop should be quiet. She should be a teacher.

Use plain forms before はず. な-adjectives need な; nouns need の. These expectations depend on the speaker’s grounds.

Food pairing and taste

Learn how to describe a food and drink pairing.

チーズにワインがよく合います。

Wine goes well with cheese.

Noun に noun が合う describes a pairing: チーズにワインが合う, wine goes well with cheese. Adding は after に focuses or contrasts the pairing: チーズにはワインが合う. 合う（あう）means fit or go well with; 会う（あう）means meet a person.
