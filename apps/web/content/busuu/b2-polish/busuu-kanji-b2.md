## Limitations

- Readings, meanings and example words were read from the kanji intro screens of lessons that were restarted and completed on 2026-10-08; kanji from lessons not yet recorded stay null.
- The kanji glyph itself is drawn inside an animated media panel on each intro screen and is not in the page text; the glyph for each intro was identified from the lesson label, the readings and the example words.
- Listening and audio-only prompts cannot be heard by the recorder, so those answers were guesses; wrong first attempts are recorded together with the corrected answer Busuu showed.
- Screen indexes count in the order seen; a lesson can have two activities (the second activity URL started at exercise_2 and its exercise_1 was never displayed). Wrong answers are re-queued as retry screens at the end.
- Post-lesson screens (ad interstitial, premium upsell, daily challenges, league, streak) are listed per lesson but are not lesson content; the streak-goal buttons were not pressed.
- Busuu does not show stroke count or JLPT level.
